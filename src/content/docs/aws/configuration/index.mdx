This section is about configuring the LocalStack emulator beyond its default settings. Out of the box, LocalStack runs with sensible defaults that suit most everyday use, but real-world projects often need more. You may want to tune environment variables to match the way your application talks to AWS, point traffic through a custom network setup, switch container images, change how authentication and credentials are handled, or extend the emulator with additional packages and SDK helpers.

The pages here describe the configuration knobs available to you and walk through the supporting components, including the LocalStack Console, the LocalStack SDKs, Extensions, the DNS server, and the other utilities you can layer on top of a running emulator to fit it to your environment.
